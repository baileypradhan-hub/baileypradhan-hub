## Featured Projects

### [Whose Fed Is It Anyway? Political Cycles and Monetary Policy Independence](https://github.com/baileypradhan-hub/fed-independence-taylor-rule)
Sole-authored. Built a Taylor Rule and Phillips Curve framework across 71 years of monthly Fed
data (861 observations, 3 FRED series) to test whether the Fed's rate decisions have
historically deviated from a rules-based benchmark during politically pressured periods:
Burns, Volcker, and Powell II. Used the framework to evaluate a Warsh-led Fed.

### [The December 2018 FOMC Decision: Market Reactions & Empirical Issues](https://github.com/baileypradhan-hub/fomc-dec2018-market-reactions)
Co-authored (my section), written Spring 2026. Rebuilt the 10-year/2-year Treasury spread from
historical data and found it had compressed to 0.12 percentage points ahead of the Fed's
December 2018 meeting. Analyzed the S&P 500's reaction to Chairman Powell's press conference,
and used the James-Klaus framework to explain why the Phillips Curve stayed flat even at a
50-year unemployment low.

### [Between a Rock and a Hard Place: Botswana's Escape from the Resource Curse](https://github.com/baileypradhan-hub/botswana-diamond-resource-curse)
Co-authored (my section), written Spring 2026. Ran the Granger causality testing behind the
paper's central finding: mineral revenue predicts poverty reduction only after a two-year lag
(p = 0.000), while diamond production itself does not Granger-cause GDP growth. Evidence of an
enclave economy.

### [The V-Shaped Path: Bridging the TFP Gap in Benin](https://github.com/baileypradhan-hub/benin-guatemala-development-accounting)
Sole-authored, written Spring 2026. Applied a Caselli-style development accounting framework to
show that Benin's 2.52x income gap with Guatemala isn't a schooling problem. Human capital
reached parity by 2023. The real gap is a Total Factor Productivity collapse dating to the
1990s.
